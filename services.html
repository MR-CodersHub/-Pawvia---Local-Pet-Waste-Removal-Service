CONSISTENT QUALITY

Our 4-Step Route Procedure

Every single property visit follows our standardized protocol to guarantee safety, biosecurity, and meticulous yard cleanliness.

1

Gate & Perimeter Check

We ensure gate latches are sound, check for dogs in the yard, and send an arrival notification.

2

Double-Grid Sweep

We walk methodical horizontal and vertical passes across your lawn, flowerbeds, and fence lines.

3

Biosecurity Spray

Tools and boots are treated with EPA-registered veterinary disinfectant before closing our van.

4

Photo Gate Verification

We latch your gate securely, take a timestamped verification photo, and text it directly to your phone.
