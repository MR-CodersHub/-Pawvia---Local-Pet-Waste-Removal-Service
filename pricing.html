Additional Dogs & Yard Size Policies

We believe in transparent, straightforward pricing. Here is how dog counts and larger properties are calculated.

Additional Dog Rates

All base rates include 1 dog. Additional dogs require extra scooping time and bag weight capacity:

  • 1st Dog: Included in base price ($19/wk)
  • 2nd Dog: +$6 per visit ($25/wk total)
  • 3rd Dog: +$6 per visit ($31/wk total)
  • 4+ Dogs: +$5 for each additional dog

Yard Dimensions & Acreage

Our standard route pricing covers typical suburban backyards:

  • Standard Lot (Up to 1/4 Acre): Included in standard price
  • Medium Lot (1/4 to 1/2 Acre): +$8 per visit
  • Large Acreage (1/2 to 1 Acre): +$18 per visit
  • Properties over 1 Acre: Contact us for a custom walking route assessment
