SCIENTIFIC FACTS

The Truth About Canine Waste in Residential Lawns

Why environmental protection agencies classify pet waste in the same high-risk hazard category as toxic chemicals.

23M

Bacteria Per Gram

A single gram of dog waste contains approximately 23 million fecal coliform bacteria, capable of causing cramps, intestinal illness, and kidney failure in humans.

EPA

Non-Point Source Pollutant

The US EPA ranks pet waste in the same category as herbicides and toxic runoff, because stormwater washes backyard waste directly into local rivers and reservoirs.

10M

Tons Annually in the U.S.

American dogs produce over 10 million tons of waste each year. Professional responsible disposal ensures waste is kept out of storm sewers and recycled responsibly.

FOUR-SEASON CARE

Seasonal Yard Hygiene Guide

How yard requirements change from springtime mud to summer heat and winter frosts.

Spring Thaw

Clear accumulated winter droppings before high spring rains liquefy waste into your soil thatch.

Summer Heat

Apply bio-enzymatic spray weekly to break down ammonia crystals and prevent aggressive fly swarms.

Autumn Leaves

Perform systematic corridor passes to spot hidden waste camouflaged beneath heavy fallen foliage.

Winter Frost

Never skip winter cleanups. Cold preservation keeps bacteria alive until snow melts in spring.
