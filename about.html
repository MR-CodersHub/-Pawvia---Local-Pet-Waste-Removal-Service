OUR ORIGIN STORY

Built by Dog Owners, for Dog Owners

Like many of our clients, we adopted two high-energy rescue dogs and quickly discovered that keeping up with yard waste while managing full-time jobs and kids was an endless, stressful chore.

We noticed that standard lawn care companies wouldn't touch pet waste, and casual neighborhood scoopers were unreliable—often skipping visits, leaving gates unlatched, or dumping waste right into homeowners' domestic trash cans where it baked in the summer heat.

In 2021, Pawvia was born. We invested in hospital-grade veterinary biosecurity gear, built an automated dispatch and gate-lock photo confirmation system, and hired dedicated animal lovers who treat your property like their own. Today, we're proud to serve over 35 local communities every single week.

OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

The Values That Guide Every Visit

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of reliability, safety, and respect for your home.

Strict Biosecurity

We treat gear sanitization as a medical-level duty. Zero transfer of parasites, parvovirus, or bacteria between customer properties.

Gate Security First

A closed gate is non-negotiable. Every technician latches, tests, and photographs the fence before driving away.

Responsible Disposal

We haul all waste away to approved municipal facilities. We never leave bags in your outdoor bins to attract pests and stink.

Total Transparency

No contracts, no surprise invoices. Honest pricing with easy self-service pause or cancellation anytime.

BIO-SAFETY PROTOCOL

Hospital-Grade Disinfection on Every Route

Cross-contamination is the silent danger of pet waste removal. If a company scoops a yard with Giardia or parvovirus and walks onto your lawn without disinfecting, your dog is at severe risk.

1

Veterinary-Approved Antimicrobial Spray

All scooper rakes and collection pans are thoroughly saturated with an EPA-registered germicide before entering each new property.

2

Shoe Sole Sanitization

Techs either wear disposable boot covers or step into an antimicrobial bath before walking on your turf.

3

Heavy-Duty Biodegradable Double-Bagging

Waste is safely sealed in puncture-resistant, plant-based liners to prevent leaks and spills.
