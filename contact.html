ZERO ESCAPES GUARANTEE

Gate Access & Property Security Protocol

We take property security and canine safety as seriously as cleanliness. Here is how our technicians manage entry.

Combination & Keyed Padlocks

You can store 4-digit wheel codes or key lockbox locations encrypted in your account. Technicians scramble dials after re-locking.

Timestamped Geo-Tagged Photo

Before leaving your perimeter, our field software requires the technician to snap a photo of the latched gate, automatically sent to your phone.

Pup-Friendly Entry Checks

Technicians gently rattle the gate and whistle before unlatching, checking that curious dogs don't dart out when the gate swings open.

WEATHER RELIABILITY

Our Inclement Weather & Reschedule Policy

We work in light rain, cold weather, and breezy afternoons. Here is how extreme conditions are handled.

Rain & Showers

Normal Service

Light to moderate rain does not delay visits. We wear waterproof boots and proceed on our scheduled route day.

Severe Lightning

Same-Week Catch-Up

For technician safety, active thunderstorms cause brief route pauses. Yards are rescheduled automatically within 24–48 hours.

Heavy Snow & Freezes

Thaw Sweep

When deep snow covers lawns, we conduct a double-pass cleanup as soon as temperatures rise, with zero backdated penalty fees.
