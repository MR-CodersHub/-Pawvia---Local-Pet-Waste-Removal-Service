WHY CLEAN LAWNS MATTER

A Healthier, Safer Yard for Your Family and Pets

Dog waste is not fertilizer—it contains billions of harmful bacteria, parasites, and nitrogen salts that damage turf and endanger your household.

Disease & Parasite Defense

A single gram of dog waste harbors over 23 million coliform bacteria, hookworms, and roundworms. Routine removal stops soil contamination before parasites reach your pets or children.

Odor & Fly Control

Warm sunshine and uncollected waste quickly attract flies and create foul odors. Our scheduled pickups keep outdoor spaces fresh, pleasant, and ready for backyard barbecues.

Reclaim 2+ Hours Every Week

Never argue about whose turn it is to scoop again. Hand this messy, unpleasant chore over to our prompt professionals and enjoy your free weekend time.

Protects Grass from Lawn Burn

Canine waste contains highly acidic nitrogen compounds that burn grass roots and cause yellow dead patches. Fast removal preserves rich green lawn vitality.

No Dirty Bins Left Behind

Unlike services that bag waste and toss it in your personal municipal trash bin, we double-bag and haul everything away to approved waste facilities.

Friendly to Your Pups

Our techs are lifelong dog lovers trained in dog body language. Friendly dogs are welcome in the yard during visits, or we work while they rest indoors.
